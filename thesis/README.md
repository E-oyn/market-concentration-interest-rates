# Thesis

`Oyan_2021_thesis.pdf` is the submitted Master's thesis, with two changes made for this public copy:

1. **Title page replaced** — the original listed a home address, personal email, and university matriculation number. The replacement page keeps the title, supervisor, examiner, author name, program, and submission date, and drops the rest.
2. **Declaration-of-authorship page removed** — a required formality for university submission (signed statement of original authorship, in English and German), not needed for a public read of the thesis.

No other content was changed — all chapters, figures, tables, references, and the appendix are unmodified from the original submission.
